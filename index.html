Findings

Each finding is calculated from the data. Treat them as starting points and check video before acting on them.

    What to improve: teams

    Reference values are general starting points. Change them to match your league, age group, or goals, and everything below updates.

    Priority compares the current value with the reference. High means more than 15% off, Medium more than 5%. Proposed targets are suggestions and not guaranteed outcomes.

    What to improve: players

    Only players with enough attempts get a reading. Small samples can swing a lot from one game to the next.

    Players

    Efficiency = PTS + REB + AST + STL + BLK minus missed shots, missed free throws, and turnovers. Players who did not play are listed below instead of being evaluated. A * after a name marks a starter, and EF* is the efficiency printed on the source sheet.

    Guide

    Everything you need to prepare a file and read the results.

    File columns

    Only player and team are required. StatsCentral result sheets (PDF or pasted text, like the CESAFI ones) are recognized automatically, including quarter scores and the team stats table. Missing stat columns are treated as 0 and listed in the data checks. Combined columns such as FG as 7-15 are also understood.

    StatNames we recognize
    Playerplayer, name, athlete
    Teamteam, squad, club
    Jersey numbernumber, no, #, jersey
    Minutesmin, mins, minutes, mp (a value like 32:15 also works)
    Pointspts, points
    Reboundsreb, rebounds, trb
    Assists, steals, blocksast / assists, stl / steals, blk / blocks
    Turnovers, foulstov / to / turnovers, pf / fouls
    Field goalsfgm and fga, or one fg column like 7-15
    Three-pointerstpm and tpa, 3pm and 3pa, or one 3p column
    Free throwsftm and fta, or one ft column

    Spreadsheets use the first sheet. Word (.docx) and PDF files are read when they contain a stats table with a header row, such as Player, PTS, REB, AST. If a table has no Team column, each table is labeled with the line above it, or Team 1, Team 2. Scanned or photographed PDFs have no readable text, so upload those to Claude in chat and ask for the table. TXT files can be separated by commas, tabs, semicolons, or two or more spaces.

    How the numbers are calculated

    FG%, 3P%, FT%

    Makes divided by attempts for each shot type.

    eFG%

    (FGM + 0.5 x 3PM) / FGA. It gives extra credit for threes.

    TS%

    Points / (2 x (FGA + 0.44 x FTA)). It blends all scoring into one efficiency number.

    Turnover rate

    Turnovers as a share of possessions used: TOV / (FGA + 0.44 x FTA + TOV). Lower is better.

    Assist to turnover

    Assists divided by turnovers. Above 1.5 is a common team target.

    Free throw rate

    Free throw attempts per 100 field goal attempts. It shows how often a team gets to the line.

    Questions

    Where do the reference values come from?

    They are general starting points, not official standards. Edit them on the Analyzer page to match your level of play.

    Why does a player have no improvement plan?

    There were too few attempts for a reliable read. It takes at least 6 field goal attempts, 3 three-point attempts, or 3 free throw attempts.

    Why do some stats say n/a?

    That stat needs attempts that were zero or missing in the file. Nothing is guessed.

    Is my data stored anywhere?

    No. The file is read in your browser and is never sent to a server.

    Can it replace watching video?

    No. Statistics show what happened, and video shows why. Findings are labeled as starting points for that reason.

    About Us

    Hoops Analyzer exists to turn a pile of box score numbers into a short list of things a team can actually work on.

    Our mission

    Give coaches, players, and team managers a clear, honest picture of each game and a practical plan for the next one.

    Evidence first

    We do not invent strengths or weaknesses. If the data cannot support a claim, the page says more information is needed.

    Realistic targets

    Targets are proposals with a review period. They are goals to aim for and never promised results.

    Transparent data

    Source values stay as they were uploaded. Calculated values are labeled, and questionable entries are flagged.

    Private by design

    Everything runs in your browser. Files are not uploaded or stored.
